WP-1.4 — Review Scheduler + Streaks

The shipped record for the WP-1.4 work package (tracker tasks wp14t01–wp14t03, RC-09): a pure SM-2 spaced-repetition scheduler that never fabricates streak activity, an honest review-queue card, and permission-gated daily reminders with the school-LAN limitation disclosed.

wp14t01 ✓

SM-2 scheduler

AC: review scheduling is deterministic and derived from real completion accuracy.

What was implemented

  • • src/lib/spacedRepetition.ts — pure SM-2: quality = round(accuracy × 5) from REAL correct_count/total_count; q<3 resets to a 1-day interval (ease factor survives — a lapse is scheduling noise, never a verdict); ease clamped ≥ 1.3; UTC-date math.

Proof

  • • 9 unit tests.
