Shared-library model

AC: content attribution and remix rules documented; contributors credited

What was implemented

  • • §3 attribution & remix rules: the allowed-license allowlist, attribution required for third-party rows, SA (share-alike) and NC (non-commercial) honored, credit rendered in the UI on every card and lesson page.
  • • §4 shared-library model: partner school-LAN bundles ride the same license-checked export + cache-warm path the QR pilot uses — additive, never extractive (no learner data changes hands).
  • • §5 contributor credits: every activity stores its author; the platform never replaces a third-party author's credit with its own; the oer.import audit trail is the permanent provenance record.

Proof

  • • Rules enforced in code: OERAllowedLicenses + oer_service_impl.go row checks; UI credit lines in learning/page.tsx + learning/[id]/page.tsx.
