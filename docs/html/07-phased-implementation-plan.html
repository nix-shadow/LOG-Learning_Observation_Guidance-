Phase 0 · Foundation

weeks 1–4

Trust, privacy and engineering hygiene. This phase is deliberately first: it is cheap, it de-risks every later phase, and it hardens the surface real classrooms will touch. RC-05 (privacy), C5/C6 (state + accessibility), C1 (audit logs), and the queue-integrity guarantees from the sync layer all land here.

WP-0.1 Privacy pack RC-05 GDPR-style rights for Nepali learners and guardians ▾
WP-0.2 Honesty & state hygiene C5 · C1 No invented numbers, no fabricated fallbacks ▾
WP-0.3 Accessibility & bundle C6 Low-bandwidth and low-vision classrooms ▾
WP-0.4 Engineering process process CI, lint, health probes ▾

Phase 1 · Learning Engine

weeks 5–14

The core learning loop: correct per-learner status, a practice engine that actually teaches, a bilingual shell, retention mechanics and a moderator experience that makes teachers effective from day one. RC-01, RC-02, RC-03 and RC-09 all land here.

WP-1.1 Per-learner status engine RC-01 Honest progress, per learner, per activity ▾
WP-1.2 Practice engine + SEE-first content RC-02 Quiz correctness, explanations, Nepal's exam reality ▾
WP-1.3 Bilingual shell i18n Nepali-first where it matters ▾
WP-1.4 Spaced repetition & streaks RC-09 Retention without pressure ▾
WP-1.5 Moderator onboarding RC-03 Teachers effective from day one ▾

Phase 2 · Teacher & Parent

weeks 15–24

Connecting the adults around the learner: a parent portal (RC-04), a structured support funnel (RC-06), an honest gradebook (RC-08) and a reconnect digest that makes offline sync visible instead of silent.

WP-2.1 Parent portal RC-04 Progress visibility for guardians ▾
WP-2.2 Who-to-call support workflow RC-06 From "it's broken" to a named person ▾
WP-2.3 Gradebook honesty RC-08 Real grades, or honestly none ▾
WP-2.4 Reconnect digest offline Make sync visible, never silent ▾

months 6–9

Content depth and ecosystem reach: OER integration (RC-07), full SEE coverage, the QR pilot (RC-10), Nepal Sign Language and accessibility content (RC-12), and formal partnership lanes (RC-11).
